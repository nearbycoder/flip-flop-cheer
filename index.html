Skills

  1. Hold O to dip.
  2. Press P + W together to jump.

  1. Hold O, add W and lean back (~½ sec).
  2. Press P + W to jump onto your hands.
  3. When your hands touch, tap Q. Let go to land.

Hold the big buttons with your thumbs — Q W on the left, O P on the right. Slide a thumb between them to switch fast. ↺ restarts.
