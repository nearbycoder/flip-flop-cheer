Flip Flop Cheer

Like QWOP — but tumbling. You control her hips, arms and legs. Land skills on your feet; anything else is a FLOP!

W

Back Tuck

  1. Hold O to dip.
  2. Press P+W together to jump & throw your arms.
  3. Switch to Q to tuck… then let go to open up and land.

Back Handspring

  1. Hold O to sit, then add W and lean back (about half a second).
  2. Press P+W to jump back onto your hands.
  3. The moment your hands touch, tap Q to snap down. Let go to land.

Combos

Land and go again quickly to chain skills — each one in a combo multiplies its points. Stand still to stick it and bank the combo (+50).

Phone: hold the big buttons — slide your thumb between Q↔W or O↔P.
Controller: LB/X = Q · LT/Y = W · RB/A = O · RT/B = P · Start = restart
Keyboard: Q W O P · R or Space = restart · H = help
